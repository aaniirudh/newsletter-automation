# Brand Guidelines — AI Systems Newsletter

## Colors
- Primary: #1A1A2E (Deep Navy) — headers, backgrounds
- Accent: #E94560 (Vibrant Red-Pink) — CTA buttons, highlights
- Background: #F5F5F5 (Light Grey) — email body
- Text: #2D2D2D (Near Black) — body copy
- Muted: #888888 (Medium Grey) — captions, footers

## Typography
- Headers: Poppins, Bold (use Google Fonts CDN fallback: sans-serif)
- Body: Inter, Regular, 16px (fallback: Arial, sans-serif)
- Line height: 1.6
- Max content width: 680px

## Logo
- File: brand_assets/AIS.PNG
- Placement: Top-center of every newsletter
- Width: 180px, auto height

## Tone
- Professional but approachable
- Data-driven and insightful
- Concise — respect the reader's time
- No hype language; cite real sources

## Newsletter Structure (in order)
1. Logo header (navy background, centered)
2. Full-width header image
3. Issue date + edition number
4. Featured story (150–200 words, with source)
5. 2–3 secondary stories (50–80 words each)
6. "What This Means For You" section (practical takeaway)
7. CTA button — accent color (#E94560), white text
8. Footer — muted grey, unsubscribe link, copyright
